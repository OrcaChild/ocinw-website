# Image Credits

All photographs below were sourced from Wikimedia Commons and are either in the public domain (US government works, Library of Congress) or released under CC0 or a Creative Commons attribution license. Photos under CC BY or CC BY-SA carry an on-page credit line via the `imageCredit` frontmatter field. Landing page images have no caption slot, so only public domain and CC0 photos are used there.

Every image was resized to a maximum of 2400px (hero) or 2000px wide at JPEG quality 80 before being committed. Original files are not stored in the repository; the Commons link is the source of truth.
